AnyLab's modifications: selected the first 1,500 non-empty corpus documents in
archive order; added document and search-query prompts; generated token IDs
with the pinned Google tokenizer; packaged the 3,000 resulting rows with 12
synthetic edge-case rows as deterministic compressed JSON. Selected document rows
were also included in the reference JSON files. Original corpus text is unchanged.
The original corpus supplies document IDs, title and text, but no per-post
author field. We preserve the supplied text and links. Source document IDs and
reference-row mappings are in fixtures/source-map.json.
